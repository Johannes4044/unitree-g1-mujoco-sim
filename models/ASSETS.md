# Robot model assets: provenance and licences

`g1_pick_place.xml` and the 122 meshes in `assets/` are **vendored**: they are committed to
this repository rather than fetched at setup time, so one `git clone` (or one zip) is enough
to get the simulator running on a machine with no network. They were generated by
`scripts/build_scene.py` from the sources below, each fetched at a pinned commit. Every file
in `assets/` comes from exactly one row. All sources are permissively licensed, and the full
licence texts are in [`../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

The upstream sources themselves are *not* vendored (they are large and only needed to
regenerate the model). `scripts/build_scene.py` takes their locations from `$MENAGERIE`,
`$REVO2` and `$UNITREE_EXTRA` and refuses to run if they are not set — see its docstring.

The scenario parts in `objects/` and the composed scenes in `scenarios/` are this project's
own (Apache-2.0); they reference the meshes above through `meshdir="../assets"`.

Sensor poses (D435i, Mid-360, head module, D455) are numeric constants taken from Unitree's
`g1_29dof_mode_15_with_dex1_1.urdf` and `g1_comp.urdf` in `unitree_ros` (BSD-3-Clause).

## Changes made to third-party files

- Meshes: decimated with `fast-simplification` to at most `VISUAL_MAX_VERTS` (2500) vertices, or
  `COLLISION_MAX_VERTS` (800) for collision-only meshes, and rewritten by `trimesh` as binary STL
  under the mesh's name in the model.
- Revo 2: the URDFs are compiled to MJCF by MuJoCo. The root link `<side>_base_link` is renamed
  `<side>_hand_base_link`; geoms and meshes get the names described above. Everything else (link
  and joint names, poses, limits, inertials) is as in the URDF. Actuators, couplings, contact and
  joint settings are added by us.
- Menagerie G1: the Dex3 hands' bodies, geoms and actuators are removed and the Revo 2 hands are
  attached at the wrist; the keyframe is rebuilt for the new joint layout.

## Not used, must not ship

- **`BrainCoTech/brainco-description`** (its `revo2_system/mjcf/revo2_*.xml` was used by earlier
  builds): no LICENSE file, and the README says "License information will be provided with the
  public release. Please review the accompanying license before redistributing or modifying these
  assets." It is not cleared for redistribution. The 71 mesh files that MJCF referenced are
  byte-identical (sha256) to `revo2_description`'s, and its kinematics match the 1.0.0 URDF, so
  moving to the URDF changed no joint or actuator name, order, limit or fingertip pose. Its link
  inertias differed (larger than the URDF's by 2x to 10x); the model now uses the URDF's.
- **`revo2_description` 1.1.0 / 1.1.1** (`3341c11`, `abafd5f`, Aug 2026): they move the thumb
  metacarpal zero by 3 deg, add +9 / +6 deg origin offsets on the thumb MCP/PIP, change the limits
  (thumb 89 / 60 / 66 deg, finger distal 97 deg) and the mimic ratios (1.0945, 1.131, 1.267, 1.252,
  1.271). That changes what a recorded hand joint angle means, so the pin stays at 1.0.0 until
  the dataset layout is migrated deliberately. `build_scene.py` asserts the 1.0.0 contract.
- `unitree_ros` `robots/g1_description/meshes/head_servo_link.STL` and `xl330_link.STL` (added in
  `205bf9bad5`, BSD-3-Clause): `build_scene.py` does not reference them. The head module is drawn
  with primitives.
- Menagerie `realsense_d435i/`: `D435_XML` is defined in `build_scene.py` and never loaded, so
  nothing from it ends up in the model.
